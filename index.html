About Us

Reinventing construction from the ground up

We combine cutting-edge modular technology with world-class design to build structures that are faster, greener, and more cost-effective.

A global construction technology company

Headquartered in Europe with operations across the EU and United States, instaBuild has pioneered a new approach to construction. Our panelized and modular building systems allow us to deliver projects in a fraction of the time required by traditional methods — without sacrificing quality, design, or sustainability.

From POP-UP communities deployed in under 30 days to multi-story residential complexes, we're proving that the future of construction is offsite, modular, and intelligent.

Our Mission

To build smarter, greener, and faster while making a lasting impact on communities.

We exist to eliminate the friction between vision and reality. By combining advanced modular technology with transparent processes, we make construction accessible, predictable, and extraordinary.

Our Vision

A world where every community has access to high-quality, sustainable housing — delivered in weeks, not years.

We envision cities transformed by modular innovation — where buildings are assembled with precision, waste is eliminated, and every structure contributes positively to its environment.
