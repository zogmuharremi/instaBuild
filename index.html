About Us

Construction, reimagined

We didn't set out to build a company. We set out to fix an industry — one that's been stuck in the past for too long. Modular technology is how we deliver on that promise.

A technology company that builds homes

Headquartered in Europe with operations spanning the EU and United States, instaBuild has pioneered a completely new approach to construction. Our panelized and modular building systems are precision-engineered in climate-controlled factories — not exposed to weather, delays, or the inconsistencies of traditional on-site building.

Our Mission: To build smarter, greener, and faster — making a lasting, positive impact on every community we touch. Our Vision: A world where high-quality sustainable housing is available to everyone, delivered in weeks instead of years.

Our Construction Process

From vision to keys — in 5 steps

A battle-tested process that transforms months of complexity into days of clarity. Every project follows this same proven path.

1

Discovery

We learn your vision, requirements, and budget. Initial designs and detailed estimates produced in days, not weeks.

2

Design

3

Manufacture

Sustainability & Innovation

Innovation Center

Where the future of construction takes shape

Our dedicated Innovation Center is the research and development engine behind instaBuild's technology advantage. Here, our engineers and designers develop new modular systems, test next-generation sustainable materials, and continuously refine manufacturing processes — ensuring every project benefits from the very latest advances in construction science and engineering.

Sustainability

Net-positive by design, not by accident

Sustainability is engineered into every square meter we build. Our offsite manufacturing process reduces material waste by up to 60%. Our panelized systems exceed LEED Gold energy performance standards. And because our buildings are modular, they can be reconfigured, expanded, or relocated instead of demolished — extending their useful life indefinitely. We use responsibly sourced materials, integrate renewable energy systems, and design for passive heating and cooling from day one.
