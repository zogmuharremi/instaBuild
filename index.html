About instaBuild

Construction, reimagined

We didn't set out to build a company. We set out to fix an industry. Modular technology is how we do it.

A technology company that builds

Headquartered in Europe with operations spanning the EU and United States, instaBuild pioneered panelized systems that slash delivery times without compromise. Every component is precision-engineered indoors — no weather, no delays, no inconsistency.

Mission: Build smarter, greener, faster. Vision: High-quality sustainable housing for everyone — in weeks, not years.
