About instaBuild

Construction, reimagined

We didn't set out to build a company. We set out to fix an industry — one that's been broken for too long. Modular technology is how we do it.

A technology company that builds

Headquartered in Europe with operations spanning the EU and United States, instaBuild pioneered panelized and modular building systems that slash delivery times without compromising quality. Every component is precision-engineered in climate-controlled factories — not exposed to weather, delays, or inconsistency.

Our Mission: To build smarter, greener, and faster — making a lasting impact on every community we touch. Our Vision: A world where high-quality sustainable housing is available to everyone, delivered in weeks, not years.
