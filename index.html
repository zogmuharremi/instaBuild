About Us

Construction, finally disrupted

We didn't start a company. We started a movement — to prove homes can be built faster, smarter, and more beautifully than anyone imagined.

Born from frustration. Built for speed.

After decades watching traditional construction fail — blown budgets, endless delays, unpredictable quality — our founders built instaBuild to prove there's a better way. Every component is precision-engineered in climate-controlled factories.

Mission: Build smarter, greener, faster. Vision: High-quality sustainable housing for everyone — in weeks, not years.
