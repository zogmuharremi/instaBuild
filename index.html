FAQ

Questions you might have

How is modular different from prefab?

Prefab = individual components built offsite. Modular = entire room-sized modules manufactured complete with finishes and systems. It's the difference between ingredients and a finished meal.

What does a POP-UP community cost?

POP-UP units start at approximately $45,000 for 28m², fully finished. Pricing scales with size and quantity.

Can buildings be customized?

Absolutely. Panelized system = engineered foundation, but every project gets custom exterior finishes, interior layouts, and window configurations.

How do permits and zoning work?

Full regulatory compliance verification before manufacturing. Our engineers ensure your building meets all codes — automatically.

What warranty is included?

10-year structural warranty + 2-year comprehensive finish warranty. IoT-tracked manufacturing history included.
