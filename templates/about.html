About Us

Reinventing construction
from the ground up

We combine modular technology with world-class design to build structures that are faster, greener, and more cost-effective.

A global construction technology company

Headquartered in Europe with operations across the EU and United States, instaBuild has pioneered a new approach to construction. Our panelized and modular building systems deliver projects in a fraction of the time — without sacrificing quality, design, or sustainability.

From POP-UP communities deployed in 30 days to multi-story residential complexes, we're proving that the future of construction is offsite, modular, and intelligent.

Our Mission

To build smarter, greener, and faster while making a lasting impact on communities.

We eliminate the friction between vision and reality. Combining advanced modular technology with transparent processes, we make construction accessible, predictable, and extraordinary.

Our Vision

A world where every community has access to high-quality, sustainable housing — in weeks, not years.

We envision cities transformed by modular innovation — buildings assembled with precision, waste eliminated, every structure contributing to its environment.
